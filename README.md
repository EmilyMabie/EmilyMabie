## Hi there, internet community! 👋

My name is **Emily Mabie** (she/her), and I'm a **Sr. AI Automation Engineer for HR** at [Zapier](https://zapier.com/).

I build AI-powered automation solutions for HR teams — from onboarding and recruiting workflows to AI fluency programs — and help enterprise organizations rethink how they work with AI. I'm also a frequent contributor to industry conversations on AI's impact on the workforce, with commentary featured in [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/lots-of-businesses-are-replacing-workers-with-ai-agents-but-its-not-a-simple-swap), [Deel](https://www.deel.com/blog/ai-training-guide/), [People Managing People](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/hr-operations/ai-transformation-for-mid-market-companies/), and [TechStrong TV](https://techstrong.tv/).

Before this, I spent 2+ years as a **Sr. Learning Designer** at Zapier focused on manager enablement — building scalable programs for goal-setting, feedback, performance management, and AI fluency across a fully remote workforce. I started my career as an **Instructional Designer** at Coding Dojo, where I designed curriculum, led retention initiatives, and founded the org's first LGBTQIA+ affinity group.
